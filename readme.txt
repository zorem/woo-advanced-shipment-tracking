== Description ==

**Trusted by 80,000+ WooCommerce stores worldwide.** Advanced Shipment Tracking (AST) is the #1 WooCommerce shipment tracking plugin. Add tracking numbers to orders and automatically share them with customers — stopping "Where is my order?" support tickets before they start and keeping shoppers informed from checkout to delivery.

When you ship an order, your customer instantly gets a tracking link in their order email and on their **My Account > Orders** page. No external accounts. No complex setup. No monthly fees.

**HPOS Compatible** · **Block Checkout Compatible** · **1010+ Carriers** · **Free & Open Source**

= Why 80,000+ Stores Choose AST =

Whether you ship 5 orders a day or 5,000, AST fits your workflow without adding complexity.

* **Add Tracking Numbers Instantly** — Add one or multiple tracking numbers per order from the Edit Order page or the order list. Takes seconds.
* **"Shipped" & "Partially Shipped" Statuses** — Replace the default "Completed" status with "Shipped" and use "Partially Shipped" for split shipments, so customers always know the status of their order.
* **Customizable Tracking Widget** — A responsive tracking widget appears in order emails and on My Account > Orders. Customize colors, layout, and content with a live preview customizer — no code required.
* **1010+ Shipping Carriers Worldwide** — Pre-configured tracking links for USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, Royal Mail, Australia Post, Canada Post, Delhivery, ePacket, La Poste, Correos, Japan Post, China Post, and hundreds more. [View the full carrier list](https://docs.zorem.com/docs/ast-free/shipping-carriers/#shipping-carriers-list-options/).
* **Shipment Tracking REST API** — Let third-party shipping services, ERPs, and label generators push tracking data directly to your orders via the WooCommerce REST API. [API documentation](https://docs.zorem.com/docs/ast-free/add-tracking-to-orders/shipment-tracking-api/).
* **Bulk CSV Import** — Upload a CSV to add tracking numbers to hundreds of orders at once. Perfect for high-volume stores and dropshippers. [CSV import guide](https://docs.zorem.com/docs/ast-free/add-tracking-to-orders/csv-import/).
* **Works With Your Existing Shipping Tools** — Compatible with ShipStation, WooCommerce Shipping, Ordoro, Royal Mail Click & Drop, Sendcloud, Printful, AliExpress dropshipping, and all major label generators.
* **HPOS & Block Checkout Ready** — Fully compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (custom order tables) and the WooCommerce Cart and Checkout blocks.
* **PayPal Tracking Ready** — AST stores tracking data in the correct structure for PayPal syncing. Automated PayPal tracking sync (to release holds and reduce disputes) is available in AST PRO.
* **Built for Performance** — Lightweight, optimized code. Zero measurable impact on page load speed, Core Web Vitals, or checkout performance.

= How It Works =

1. Install and activate AST — it takes under 2 minutes.
2. Select your default shipping carriers from the settings page.
3. Add a tracking number to an order — manually, via CSV, or through the REST API.
4. AST automatically includes the tracking info and a clickable "Track" link in the customer's order email and My Account page.

No complex setup. No external accounts. It just works.

== Installation ==

**From the WordPress plugin directory (recommended):**

1. Go to **Plugins > Add New** in your WordPress admin and search for "Advanced Shipment Tracking".
2. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
3. Navigate to **WooCommerce > Shipment Tracking** to select your default shipping carriers and configure settings.
4. Open any order, add a tracking number and shipping carrier — the tracking info will appear in the customer's order email and My Account page automatically.

**Manual installation:**

Upload the `woo-advanced-shipment-tracking` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/` and activate through the Plugins menu.

**Migrating from WooCommerce Shipment Tracking (official)?**
AST automatically detects and migrates all existing tracking data when you activate it. No manual work needed.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How is AST different from the official WooCommerce Shipment Tracking extension? =

AST is built for store owners who want a real fulfillment workflow, not just a basic tracking link. Compared to the official WooCommerce Shipment Tracking extension, AST adds: 1010+ pre-configured shipping carriers (vs ~50), a customizable tracking widget with live preview, a complete Shipped / Partially Shipped fulfillment workflow, bulk CSV import, a Shipment Tracking REST API, and automatic data migration. AST is free, open source, and trusted by 80,000+ active WooCommerce stores.

= I'm migrating from WooCommerce Shipment Tracking (by WooCommerce). Will my data carry over? =

Yes. When you activate AST, it automatically detects and migrates existing shipment tracking data from the official WooCommerce Shipment Tracking extension. Your customers won't lose access to any tracking information.

= How do I add a tracking number to a WooCommerce order? =

Open any order from **WooCommerce > Orders**. You'll see a Shipment Tracking panel. Select your shipping carrier, enter the tracking number, and save. The tracking info and a clickable "Track" link will appear in the customer's order confirmation email and on their My Account > Orders page immediately.

= Where do my customers see their tracking information? =

Tracking details appear in two places: in the order status email (when the order is marked as Shipped or Completed) and on the customer's My Account > View Order page. Guest customers can also view tracking by entering their email and order ID on the WooCommerce order tracking page.

= Can I add multiple tracking numbers to one order? =

Yes. You can add as many tracking numbers as needed to a single order — for example, when an order ships in multiple packages. All tracking entries will be displayed to your customer in their order email and on My Account.

= Does AST work with ShipStation? =

Yes. AST integrates with ShipStation. In AST PRO, tracking numbers from ShipStation shipments are automatically imported to the matching WooCommerce order as soon as the label is created — no manual copy-paste needed. In AST Free, you can import ShipStation tracking numbers using the built-in CSV import tool.

= Does AST work with WooCommerce Shipping (the official WooCommerce label tool)? =

Yes. AST PRO integrates directly with WooCommerce Shipping so tracking numbers from printed labels are automatically added to the order. In AST Free, you can add the tracking number manually or via CSV.

= Does AST integrate with PayPal tracking? =

The free version stores tracking data in the correct WooCommerce metadata structure so it's PayPal-ready. Automatic synchronization of tracking to PayPal transactions — which releases payment holds faster and reduces "Item Not Received" disputes — is available in [AST PRO](https://www.zorem.com/product/woocommerce-advanced-shipment-tracking/).

= Can I use AST for dropshipping (AliExpress, CJ Dropshipping, Spocket, etc.)? =

Yes. AST is widely used by WooCommerce dropshipping stores. Import tracking numbers from your supplier's CSV file, or use the REST API to push tracking from third-party fulfillment platforms. The 1010+ supported carriers include all major dropshipping logistics providers — ePacket, Yanwen, Yun Express, China Post, 4PX, and others.

= Is AST compatible with HPOS (High-Performance Order Storage)? =

Yes. AST has fully declared HPOS compatibility and works with WooCommerce's High-Performance Order Storage (custom order tables) for fast performance on high-volume stores.

= Does AST work with WooCommerce's Block Checkout? =

Yes. AST is compatible with the WooCommerce Block Checkout. Tracking information displays correctly in block-based email templates and on the My Account page regardless of whether you use the classic shortcode checkout or the block checkout.

= Can I customize how the tracking widget looks in emails? =

Yes. AST includes a built-in live preview customizer where you can control the design, layout, colors, and content of the tracking widget in emails and on the My Account page — no coding required. See the [tracking widget customization guide](https://docs.zorem.com/docs/ast-free/setup-configuration/customize-the-email-notifications/#customize-the-tracking-widget).

= Can I assign tracking numbers to specific products in an order? =

Item-level tracking (assigning tracking numbers to specific line items and quantities) is available in [AST PRO](https://www.zorem.com/product/woocommerce-advanced-shipment-tracking/).

= My shipping carrier isn't on the list. Can I add a custom carrier? =

AST includes 1010+ predefined carriers. If yours is missing, you can suggest it on the [feature request board](https://feedback.zorem.com/ast) or the [support forum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/woo-advanced-shipment-tracking/). Adding custom carriers with your own name, logo, and tracking URL is available in [AST PRO](https://www.zorem.com/product/woocommerce-advanced-shipment-tracking/).

= Can I import tracking numbers in bulk? =

Yes. Use the built-in [CSV import tool](https://docs.zorem.com/docs/ast-free/add-tracking-to-orders/csv-import/) to add tracking numbers to multiple orders at once. Each row in the CSV corresponds to one tracking entry. AST PRO also supports automated scheduled imports via FTP/SFTP.

= Does AST send automatic delivery notifications to customers? =

AST sends a tracking notification when the order status changes to Shipped or Completed. For automatic real-time delivery updates at every carrier milestone (in transit, out for delivery, delivered), install [TrackShip for WooCommerce](https://wordpress.org/plugins/trackship-for-woocommerce/).

= Does AST work with multi-vendor marketplaces like Dokan or WCFM? =

Yes. AST is compatible with Dokan, WCFM Marketplace, and Product Vendors. Vendors can add their own tracking numbers to their orders, and customers receive tracking information through the standard WooCommerce email and My Account flows.

= Is there a REST API to add tracking numbers programmatically? =

Yes. External shipping services, ERPs, and fulfillment tools can use the [AST Shipment Tracking API endpoint](https://docs.zorem.com/docs/ast-free/add-tracking-to-orders/shipment-tracking-api/) to create, update, and delete tracking entries on WooCommerce orders.

= Will AST slow down my WooCommerce store? =

No. AST is built for performance. It loads tracking assets only on admin order pages and on the customer-facing tracking widget. There is no measurable impact on page load speed, Core Web Vitals, or checkout performance.

= Does AST support custom order numbers? =

Yes. AST is compatible with popular custom order number plugins. You can use custom order numbers in the CSV import. See the [compatibility list](https://docs.zorem.com/docs/ast-free/compatibility/) for supported plugins.
